Summary Of Project

Bite is a spray painting project where I stole a graffiti artist’s alphabet and turned it into the word LUCKY, taking it from sketch to digital plan to finished wall.

Project Specifications

  • Artist’s alphabet: ESOW.

  • The word: LUCKY, in 5 colours.

  • Tools: Photoshop and spray paint.

  • Sprayed between 21st and 28th October.

  • AI use: how AI was (and wasn’t) used.

The Task

This project is a fun one! I was given the task of stealing a graffiti artist’s alphabet and making it into a chosen word, which in this case was LUCKY. I had to sketch it out, plan it out, and then spray it out.

The graffiti artist I was given was ESOW, a Japanese artist who doesn’t specifically do spray painting but is a jack of all trades when it comes to creating art. His style has big, bold lettering with little characters in the letters, so it was really fun to do.

Sketching and Planning

The first thing I did was find out what his alphabet was. This was the image that was supplied.

When I first saw it, I was very excited, as I felt it was a fun and very expressive typeface. With that, I did a sketch of what the word would look like. The chosen word was LUCKY.

After the sketch was done, I made a digital painting in Photoshop, and then placed it on the wall to get a visual reference of what it should look like.

Then the project came to a bit of a standstill, as it was a class project and not a solo one. I had to wait a month before I was allowed to spray and paint, which I did from 21st to 28th October.

Spraying & Painting

On the Saturday, when the weather was good, I woke up around 9am to start spraying my piece. I started by doing a black outline of the shape of the word LUCKY.

I then started to paint inside the outline. I chose a mint green and a light blue with a grey gradient in the middle to separate them, as I felt it worked best with the word and the artist’s style. I also wanted to challenge myself: instead of using one colour, I wanted to use five. The colours were max green, costa brava blue, black, white and sputnik grey.

Reflection

This was the first time I ever spray painted, and I’m proud of what I’ve done, but I do have my gripes with it and I know I can do better. For starters, the final piece looks different to the original plan. I would argue it looks better than the original because of the enlarged details in the letters, but it really should look like what I originally planned.

The gradient in the middle doesn’t fade in well with the green and the blue. I struggled a lot trying to get it right and I still think it looks okay, but next time I go spray painting, that is something I’ll think about while spraying.

Some feedback I got was that the lines weren’t very clean and were a bit rough, so that is something I would definitely think about on my next spray project, which I hope is soon. I think I want to do a mural next time, so that may be something I do in my own time.

But enough of being hard on myself. I do think I did a good job for someone who had never touched a spray can in his life, and this project shows the potential I have for my next spray piece.
