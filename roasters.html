Summary Of Project

Roasters is a mini project I set myself over the summer, for fun and to explore more branding. It’s a brand identity for a fictional coffee company in Belfast, brought to life across cups, packaging, menus, signage and merch, so it feels like a real place you could walk into.

Project Specifications

  • The project ran for 2 weeks.

  • A self-initiated summer project.

  • A coffee brand set in Belfast.

  • Packaging, menus, signage and merch.

The Brief

I wanted to treat this like a real client project, so I asked ChatGPT to write me a brief, as if a real coffee chain had sent it.

The brief was to create a brand identity for a coffee company based in Belfast, and to design products that showcase the brand.

The Research

I researched by visiting coffee shops and restaurants around me, looking at what made their branding work:

  • Bunsen: a slick menu printed like a business card.
  • Fidela: their own branded ground coffee to take home.
  • Follow Coffee: bold, bright design that really stands out.

I also took inspiration from Helwit’s design style, with its monospace typography and neon colours.

The Design

I focused on a confident, minimal system that could flex across packaging, menus and signage. Monospace type keeps it utilitarian but full of character, and a neon green accent gives it energy.

Mockups did a lot of the work: I used them heavily to sell the feeling of a real place, not just a logo on a page.

The Brand in Use

To make Roasters feel real, I put the brand on everything a coffee shop would need.

Reflection

This project taught me that a solid idea and consistent presentation go a long way. Pushing the mockups is what made the brand feel like a real location.

If I had more time, as I was trying to practice using the software before my second year at Ulster. I would definitely have a motion showreel, of the brand in use and also some possibly some stop motion, as I feel that would've made the design stronger.

My favourites are the cup holder, the menu and the ground coffee packaging. I gave myself two weeks, and I’m really pleased with the result.
