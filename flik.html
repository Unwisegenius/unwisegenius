Project Specifications

  • Inspired by Twin Peaks’ Red Room.

  • Branding, a website, merch and posters.

Overview

Flik Multiplex was a summer mini project to showcase web design, branding and merchandise. Having spent years working in a cinema, I knew the place from the inside, and I wanted to design one that felt special.

The design draws on the surreal energy of Twin Peaks’ Red Room, which fits the theatrical feel of a classic cinema.

Finding the Direction

I first explored a brutalist approach with dulled colours, but it felt too flat for a cinema. A night at the movies should feel like an event.

Shifting to a David Lynch and Twin Peaks influence unlocked the right mood: surreal, bold and atmospheric. That lens shaped the colour, typography and motion.

The Design

The system centres on high-contrast visuals, confident type and theatrical compositions.

  • Colour: a deep red, like the Red Room’s curtains, paired with warm cream.
  • Pattern: the zigzag chevron of the Red Room floor frames every page.
  • Feel: textures and layouts that nod to curtains and stage lighting, so it feels like a night out.

The Website

The website is where the brand comes to life. Its pages cover what’s on, the story behind Flik and the merch shop.

Merch & Posters

Merch and posters extend the identity beyond screens. My favourite is the movie-quote poster.

Reflection

I really enjoyed the experimentation on this project, especially the movie-quote poster. Moving away from the brutalist idea taught me that a strong reference can unlock a whole brand.

I gave myself one week, so with more time, I’d design a mobile app flow and expand the merchandise line.
